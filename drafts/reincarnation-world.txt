Jiang Lin has a Game System, a lunch tray, a school uniform, and a carefully cultivated habit of being forgettable. Then a red light takes him off a school roof and drops him into a forest of screaming strangers. The Loop World introduces itself: mission worlds, protection quests, elimination runs, faction wars, and a reward shop that accepts only exchange points. Everyone else sees a quiet student with no weapons and no team. Jiang Lin sees levels, loot, and a game he has already cleared in another life. He is the System made flesh: a body that runs on hit points, a private stat sheet, an aim that locks on, and a fear response switched off like a status effect. The mission worlds are his dungeons, and he runs them like a man who has read the book—rescue the lost girl, farm the goblins, turn a zombie siege into a harvest, choose the losing side of a martial-arts war because he knows how it ends. The first mission leaves most of the strangers dead and a perfect rating in his log. The missions pay him in levels and exchange points. The Loop World collects the rest—sometimes in the lives of strangers who trusted him, always in another piece of the ordinary world. The bill is never in HP. He can still laugh, eat, and want; the System just makes sure he never has to feel the weight. He tells himself every face that survives the run is a witness, a liability, or bait. He spends his points on a bloodline, spellbooks, and crafting skills, then builds a shop out of what he makes. The Loop World is full of people who have learned the same arithmetic: team captains with bloodlines and body counts, veterans who treat newcomers as walking shields, rival Loopers who would rather kill a promising rookie than let him grow. Fang Xinyi, the school's Ice Beauty, transfers into his department to stand closer to him. Between missions he goes back to class, sells potions online, and pretends she is just another classmate. Gao Wen, an actress who runs a team of her own, offers him a seat at her table. Neither woman is content to watch from the sidelines. He refuses every team that asks, Blood Rose included, and sells them enchanted bullets instead. A man with a secret that big cannot afford witnesses, so Jiang Lin keeps his distance. But the missions are getting harder, the teams are learning what he can do, and the Loop World does not care how clever a player is. It only cares whether he comes back. The rest is just noise.
